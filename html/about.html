Our Story

Cashwell Irrigation Repair & Lighting Design is a small, owner-operated business built around the same values that guide us in everyday life: integrity, reliability, and calm problem-solving. Every system we touch gets the kind of care we’d expect for our own homes.

About twenty years ago, our founder started helping friends and neighbors with landscaping and quickly fell in love with the work. What began as a way to lend a hand slowly grew into a dedicated service focused on serving homeowners who want their yards to both work and look their best.

Customers commonly mention the same things after working with us: they show up on time, explain their findings in plain language, and recommend only what’s actually needed. There’s no hard sell — just clear options, honest pricing, and clean workmanship.

Services at a glance

We specialize in irrigation repair and low-voltage landscape lighting—with straightforward recommendations, clean work, and no surprises.

  • Irrigation troubleshooting and repairs (leaks, broken valves, wiring, etc.)
  • Landscape lighting repair, upgrades, and new installs
  • Seasonal maintenance plans available (spring start-up, mid-season checkups, winterization)
